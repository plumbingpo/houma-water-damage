Pipes that burst rank among the most frequent causes of water damage, and they can do devastating harm to a building. A single burst pipe can release 4 to 8 gallons of water per minute, which is 240 to 480 gallons per hour flooding through your walls, ceilings and floors. Pipes burst from freezing, old plumbing, too much water pressure, corrosion or a physical hit, and in every case a fast emergency response is what keeps water from ruining flooring, structural materials, building systems and your belongings.

Houma Restoration provides 24/7 emergency response for burst pipe water damage throughout Houma and surrounding areas. Following extraction, our structural drying process addresses all moisture-affected materials, including drywall, insulation, wood framing, subflooring and finished flooring. Burst pipes often damage multiple rooms and multiple levels, because water follows gravity through wall cavities, floor assemblies and ceiling spaces. Maintenance Tips for Burst Pipe Repair

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Pressure higher than 80 PSI wears on pipes and their fittings, so test yours from time to time
  • Disconnect garden hoses and shut off outdoor faucets before the first freeze
  • Wrap any bare pipe that runs through a crawl space, garage or attic without heat
  • Every few years, ask a licensed plumber to look over the system for corrosion and worn parts
  • During freezing weather, keep faucets dripping and cabinet doors open so heat can circulate
  • Aging galvanized or polybutylene plumbing should be swapped out ahead of an unexpected failure

Why Hire a Professional for Burst Pipe Repair?

What you can see after a pipe bursts is usually only part of the damage. It moves between ceiling joists, inside floor assemblies, beside electrical conduit and down wall cavities, soaking materials you might not find for days or even weeks. By then, mold may already be established in hidden spaces and structural materials may be significantly weakened. Our technicians find all the wet areas at the start by tracing migration with thermal imaging and meter readings.

Consumer tools cannot compare with the scale of professional drying and extraction equipment. Truck-mounted extractors remove water hundreds of times faster than a shop vacuum, and commercial dehumidifiers pull 10 to 20 times more moisture from the air than residential units. With only 24 to 48 hours before mold starts, you need equipment this fast and this strong.

Signs You Need Burst Pipe Repair in Houma

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • A spray or steady drip coming off pipework you can see
  • Water pooling on floors with no apparent source
  • An unusually high water bill that points to a hidden leak
  • Wet spots, bulging or staining on walls or ceilings
  • Tap water that turns brown, yellow or cloudy
  • A fixture that stops flowing when it is below freezing outside
  • Pipes coated in ice or frost when temperatures drop
